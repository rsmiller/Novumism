THE REALIZATIONS

The core foundation of this philosophy is based on realizations I have come to about the internal and external workings of the human animal on this planet. As I have grown and evolved through decades of life, I have gained wisdom and understanding through life’s hardships of my genetics, my class, my race, my education, and my existence among other people in a society I no longer recognize. I have split these realizations into those of nature, man, mans state of being, and of the society he creates.
