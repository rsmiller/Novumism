THIRD PILLAR

The third and final pillar are the Laws used to alleviate those found in the Realizations that are causing suffering. We gein to incorporate the Laws of Society as a backstop against change, philosophies of nihilism, and other destructive elements. In this section we hope to build our societies with the appropriate safeguards for longevity as laid out in the Laws of Society. We then turn these laws and look into our selves by incorporating the States of Self and Simple Living
