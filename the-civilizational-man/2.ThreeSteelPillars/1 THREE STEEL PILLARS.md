THREE STEEL PILLARS

The Three Steel Pillars is a foundational concept where we identity the root, the cause, and the path forward for the Civilizational Man. 

The Three Steel Pillars are as follows:
    1. Life is suffering.
    2. The cause of suffering is the abandonment of Perennialism in modernity creating lawlessness, disunity, war, and mental illness. The Realizations identify these aspects.
    3. The cessation of suffering can be achieved by understanding:
        ◦ Laws of Society
        ◦ Laws of Self

Many philosophers over the ages have identified suffering as the root. Life is suffering, even for the wealthy although, let’s be honest, it probably not as much as others – different sure, but not primitively. General suffering though includes birth, illness, old age, separation from friends or family, addiction, and negative emotions. Being alive is to suffer. Utopians and transhumanists capitalize on this root problem and offer solutions. 

The Civilizational Man sees this root of suffering as a departure from the perennial structures of society, the over complexity it creates, and the downstream effects it creates on the individual and the community. It is known that as a system becomes more complex that interlinking systems are stressed, and can behave poorly, or from a software engineering stance can cause bugs and failures in the business logic. Civilization is a series on interlinking systems, from the big to the individual system: you. The Realizations are a series of observations and opinions about nature, man, society, and the self that are the cause of the suffering.

 Once we have identified the biggest concepts and the largest impacts we can begin to address those issues by formulating the Laws of Society and the Laws of the Self. These Laws push back against the causes of suffering from a society level but also on an individual level as well; for society is built from the individual on up.
