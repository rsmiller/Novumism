REALIZATIONS OF MAN

When we look to man, what do we see? What does history say of man? Where is it going? What are their motivations? What does biology say? What philosophies can we build on?

I believe in Kant's ideas that all of humanity is derived through concepts. These are foundational building blocks that lead to knowledge and then to action. With that said, I explore conceptual ideas surrounding man's place in the world.
