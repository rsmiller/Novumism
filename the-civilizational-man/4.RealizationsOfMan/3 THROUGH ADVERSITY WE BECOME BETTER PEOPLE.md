THROUGH ADVERSITY WE BECOME BETTER PEOPLE

Through pain we learn to live well. There is a strange occurrence that filtered through Western society after World War II in that humans must gravitate towards comfort and that we must have comfort at all costs. 

This notion, whether planned or organic, made current generations as of this writing the most docile, mentally ill, unstable, and unable to cope with reality that has existed outside of wartime or a real global pandemic killing millions regionally. Criminals, the outcasts, can in fact be redeemed; a human redemption arc and accompanying story is inspiration for others. Redemption is possible for those willing to put in the work, but murder, attempted murder, human trafficking, child exploitation, and many others of the like are irredeemable.

What this realization does is force the family to work together to find a solution for the troubled person, whatever that solution may be. Because their actions degraded the lives of others through becoming a visible outcast, potentially forever, it is in the hope that in the majority of cases a familial unit will become more cohesive in problem solving. Over the course of a few generations the degraded family structure of the West will become healed by family members working towards generational goals.
