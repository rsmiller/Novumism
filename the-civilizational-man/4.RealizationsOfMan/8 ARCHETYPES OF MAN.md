ARCHETYPES OF MAN

Throughout the years philosophers categorized the types of man. Nietzsche in paticular wrote about these classifications but never gave them a full written distinction, only their essence. Here I am capturing the types of man I see in society and civilization. They can be seen throught history, existing today, and into the future. 

These are not class systems nor static. Individuals can move between them and embody characteristics of one or more. These archetypes exist because they embody a certian truth that can't be discarded easily.

We can cast blame on one or more of these archtyes for the failings of society but that is not what they are here for. These types are used for identification of characteristics and giving them name. These are the slave, the common man, the warrior, the revolutionary, and the aristocrat.


THE SLAVE

The slave class is one in which they are ruled predominately through their basic instincts. The slave is tamed, aimed, and fired at opponents through the use of propaganda that overrides the emotional centers of their brain. 

A whopping 15% of humans are self-aware of what they do and their impacts around them, and this is no exception for the slave class. Their information consumption may be low and/or high. The high information consumption people will be drawn to politics, messaging boards, and jobs that involve people, including jobs in technology. Low information people are again people-focused and are often found to be managerial in nature; they may consume high information at work, but outside their specialty they are often blissfully unaware.

They are often midwits in terms of intelligence, but their specialties will often give them a false sense of personal security in that department. Their religion is whatever makes them feel good or that they are doing good. Either through indoctrinating children for the sake of ideology or robbing a store during a protest, these people are often dangerous to the whole of society when not pointed in a direction to vent their tendencies.


THE COMMON MAN

The common man is referenced far more in this book than any other type. That is because the common person is the average of the average. They are the "accended" Slave in intellegence and career but could still be a Slave in other ways.

They encompass most people and their information consumption and ability to process said information varies. Their occupation varies depending on their unique characteristics, their upbringing, where they live, and a host of other attributes.

Their immune response to propaganda can be limited but it depends on lived experience, communication with others, information consumption, and IQ, all of which exist on bell curves and averages. However, there are some exceptions. We should note that during the Covid-19 pandemic the average common man can be made to abide by broad restrictive regulations if others in the herd are following authority structures.


THE WARRIOR

This archtype is an offshoot of the Common Man and is a genetic typing as much as it a choice by the person. We often see families of people who serve in the military in one degree as another, which shows this typing, but there are definately those who choose this life as a means to an end.

When I was in the Texas State Guard I was able to encounter these people up close and examine the lives they live including their career choices. It is hard to explain, but you knew people who were veterens vs the people who were there as a hobby or for other reasons. They see the world differently and project themselves accordingly.

This archtype exists in society for a reason and some may call them pawns of The Machine, but I what I found is that they have existed since the dawn of humanity, and will exist forever into the future. Despite the averages of the Common Man, their duty to the greater good is what seperates them is that they will do the dirty work at the behest of the system because that is their existence.

Many choose security jobs, protection, law enforcement, EMS, manufacturing, and work with their hands. Being a Slave, in any regard, is something most actively avoid, and they build their lives around that.


THE REVOLUTIONARY

Another offshoot of the Common Man is the revolutionary. These people see the structures and look to build or destroy them depending on their philosophy or ideology. The reason why this is a seperate architype is that they preceive various injustices in the world tha tothers do not.

As such they take on a personal or professional crusade to change society in their chosen ideology. They will utilize the Slave and the Common Man as a means to fill their ranks and fulfull thier agendas. They are not all that disimilar to the Warrior in that both will utilize their skills and tactics to achieve an objectives. Both can be used to topple governments in various forms.
