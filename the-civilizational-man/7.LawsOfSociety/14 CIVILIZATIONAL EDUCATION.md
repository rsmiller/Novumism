CIVILIZATIONAL EDUCATION

Kant identified that humanity can induce reason through personal experience. 
The adage of “if you don’t vote Left when you're young you don’t have a heart, and when you're old and don’t vote Right you don’t have a brain” is a real phenomenon. One can theorize that as a human gains more perspective and additive context to concepts they have had over many years, they are more likely to want to preserve society and ensure wealth. Whereas when you are young, and see injustice in the world, you are likely to vote for policies of redistribution, because one hasn’t had enough lived experience or gained enough conceptual context as to what the human animal is.
