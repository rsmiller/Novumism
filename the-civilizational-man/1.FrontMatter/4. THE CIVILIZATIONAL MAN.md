THE CIVILIZATIONAL MAN

Being a Civilizational Man is as much a state of being as it is an acceptance of responsibility within society and a generational duty.

It is paramount that every man and woman hold a value structure of duty, putting civilization within focus. The civilizational man has a duty hierarchy of:
1. Family
2. God
3. Civilization
4. Individual

The civilizational man’s duty is to be present for their families, to guide them, nurture them, and support them. They accept God within their being, striving to be servants of His will. They are to abide by the law and place their actions, thoughts, and desires framed as either supporting civilization or tearing it down. They are to be knowledge bearers of their trade(s), to self-improve their body, mind, and knowledge. They are to be present within their communities, through church or volunteering. 

Deconstruction and dismantling ideologies are in vogue because it’s easy; anyone can tear down a fence, but to build one takes some level of skill. The civilizational man is one of societal construction and traditionalism. This construction does not precede the actions of deconstruction but rather consists in building system and societal resilience to deconstruction.

The civilizational man’s duty hierarchy is his Ikigai, a Japanese concept of an individual’s map of meaning and purpose through a synthesis of passion, mission, and vocation. The questions the civilizational man must ask himself are: is how you treat people promoting civilization, is your, and is your career adding value to civilization? These are the types of questions that when one centers their life around your loved ones and society that need to be asked.

Utilizing the Three Steel Pillars as a cornerstone and guide from which the civilizational man conducts his life accordingly. From these Pillars form the Realizations. The Realizations of Nature are the foundations from which all build and encorporate the material and inmaterial. The Realizations of Man are obsersations of humanity existing amoungst one another in a societal perspective. The Realizations of Being are observations of humanity from a psychological perspective. Wrapping up the realizations are the Realizations of Society where we examine elements of society as a whole and some of mans creations in broader society.

These Realizations are the first layer from which our ethics and morality are built. This is where the Laws of Society and the Laws of the Self come into focus. I have found that much of humanity needs something "bigger than ones self" in their life. Many use God as a centralized focus wherein God's blessings and favor are rewards for devotion. In our morality, although I am convinced of God's existence and hierachy, we focus on the world of the here and now: civilization and its heirachy.

In my time, we have gone through a mass deconversion of religion started with a societal psyop called the "New Athiest movement." Although at the time of this writing there is a societal re-alignment towards a focus on God, there will always be those where the leap is too great. For those people I hope that having civilization as focus creates something "bigger than ones self" in their lives. For those of faith, I believe having civilization an additional focus makes the world of man, a better place for God's children.
