WRAPPING IT ALL UP

    1. Core Values
        a) Sensible Social Freedom
            1. Personal freedom is permitted within boundaries that sustain civilization.
            2. Freedom is not license to destabilize family, trust, or continuity.
            3. Private behavior is tolerated; public normalization is restricted.
        b) Economic Freedom but with Civilizational Constraints
            1. Markets exist to serve survival, not abstraction.
            2. Speculation that weakens population stability or production is curtailed.
            3. Labor, land, and capital are subordinated to long-term continuity.
    2. Collective Obligation to Civilization
        a) Civilization is an inherited trust, not an individual choice.
        b) Citizens are obligated to uphold institutions, norms, and continuity.
        c) Rights are paired explicitly with duties.

    4. Sensible Traditional Values
    5. National Isolation as Stabilization
