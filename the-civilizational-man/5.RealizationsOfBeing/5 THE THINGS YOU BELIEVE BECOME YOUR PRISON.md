THE THINGS YOU BELIEVE BECOME YOUR PRISON

We are the sum of information we know, the concepts we believe, and our physical perception of the world. Your eyes take in information and that information is then processed through the preconceptions, beliefs, and constructs you adhere to. Two people witnessing the same event may have different interpretations of that event. 

Leftists tend to take this notion and deconstruct this process and espouse that all things, and they mean all, are therefore subjective and reside only within the mind of the observer. However, people witnessing the same event may have vastly different interpretations, or there may be overlap, which is called shared reality. However still, someone's interpretation of an event can be simply wrong and have no basis in reality. Claiming that you are a dog doesn’t make you a dog even if you walk on all fours, eat from a dish, and have a tail installed – that makes you insane. There then are things that are objective regardless of an individual's delusion; thus not everything is subjective.

Books have been written about the concept of mental contagion, a virus of the mind, or as the author Gad Saad proclaims, a Parasitic Mind. What you put in your mind creates a membrane through which perception and thought filter. This in turn makes you act or react to things around you or even your own thoughts. It has been shown that people who fixate on a problem in their life become consumed by it, changing their mental state to depressed, anxiety-filled, or murderous.

You control what you believe. You’re not stuck with it; atheists turn to God or the religions turn to religion every day. Control your mind, and you control your destiny.
