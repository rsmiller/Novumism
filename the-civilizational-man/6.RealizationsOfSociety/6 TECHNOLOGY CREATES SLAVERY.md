TECHNOLOGY CREATES SLAVERY

Ted Kaczynski was right; he was just wrong about what he did. In large part he was able to project technology's advance and mapped it well enough. In the end, his early life and the psychological experiments conducted on him left him isolated and mentally ill. He projected outward and several lost their lives. In another time he would have had a million subscribers online and done podcasts and social media shows. Yet still, what he did was wrong, but his idea that technology is slavery was correct.

Today we live with electronic everything. Water pumps for our drinking water operate on 1s and 0s. Our agriculture comes from machines with GPS and onboard computers. Our medicine comes from computerized manufacturing, from precise distillation, electronic assembly lines, to packaging that is loaded on ships that utilize computers and GPS. Our world is digital, and it will go away when the Sun sends a big enough Coronal Mass Ejection our way like it has many times in the past and will in the future. Technology isn’t just a savior but a death warrant for civilizations across the globe.

This warrant when due will create a level of barbarism not seen since pre-history. Billions will die from starvation, lack of water, from lack of breathing machines, from heat, from the cold, and so many other conditions we once thought we had eradicated. The next deluge will come and wash away most everything we built. That is why I speak of perennial idealism so heavily, not because it is fashionable but because it is a stopgap against the complete erasure of our species.

This is why utopianism is so dangerous. Why techno-optimism and trans-humanism are so destructive. When God decides the time, everything we’ve built beyond the 1700s becomes inert almost instantaneously. There is no adequate preparation. Supplies will run out. People will kill for food, water, women, and ammo. Only an ideology based on perennial ideals softens the effects.
