SOCIETIES COLLAPSE WHEN DECADENCE AND COMFORT TAKE OVER

In the 1970s a team of researchers were exploring social aspects of mice when they decided to create Universe 25. Universe 25 was a mouse heaven where everything was given to the mice in abundance: food, shelter, water, and activities, and after a few generations they were all dead. The mice developed a unique social structure where female mice would climb to the top of the enclosure and spend the majority of their time cleaning themselves and eating. The male mice would stay at the bottom to fight other mice and often kill them. There were different classes of mice, where one class could mate while the others were rejected. Every once in a while the male mice would come to a nest and kill all the baby mice. The female mice would then cannibalize their offspring.

Many culprits were cited, but overcrowding wasn’t one of them because the enclosure could have sustained multiples of the peak population. The problem was that the mice refused to breed. They died because they couldn't keep up with replacement levels despite having all they needed seemingly mentally, physically, and biologically. Something switched in their rat brains and their society committed suicide on a biological level. The same is happening to our society in that decadence and a culture of embracing comfort instead of hardship and adversity will see our population plummet until we are subsumed by a better culture.
