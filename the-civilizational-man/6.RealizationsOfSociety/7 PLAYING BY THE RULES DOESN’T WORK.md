Let's say you wanted to start a bento-box style food service for your community in America. You can cook, and your food is delicious without question. If you don’t own a certified business with a kitchen, you are supposed to rent a commercial kitchen and prepare your meals. This costs money and time. If you are in the middle of nowhere Alabama, you have to drive into a large enough city that has one. So the unlawful thing to do, which a lot of people do, is cook in their kitchen and sell from there, cutting out the expensive rental fee from the commercial kitchen.

Who is going to know? If you are selling to a community of 2000 people, will someone investigate? Would the cops even care? Would the state pursue you? Probably not.
