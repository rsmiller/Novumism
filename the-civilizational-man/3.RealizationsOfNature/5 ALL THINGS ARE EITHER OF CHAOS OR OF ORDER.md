ALL THINGS ARE EITHER OF CHAOS OR OF ORDER
This realization is the summation of all other realizations. Man starts from chaos and he either embodies it or is forged into someone who embodies order. Because the mechanisms of embodiment are cyclical, societal cycles and civilizational cycles follow periods of order and periods of chaos. The realizations here, if adhered to, lay the foundation for a lasting and strong civilization.

These realizations are truths about ourselves, but once you fully comprehend what we are as laid out here, then you can see where we need to go.
