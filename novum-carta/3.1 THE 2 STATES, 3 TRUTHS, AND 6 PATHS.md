THE 2 STATES, 3 TRUTHS, AND 6 PATHS

The Way in Novum Carta is applying The Middle Way and adhering to the States, Truths, and Paths. This is condensed here as a reference but will be discussed separately.

2 STATES OF SELF

- The Hero
- The Antihero

3 TRUTHS OF LIVING

- Life is suffering
- The cause of suffering is craving, control, and judging
- The cessation of suffering can be achieved by following the 6 Paths of Self-Discipline

6 PATHS OF SELF-DISCIPLINE

- Path of Livelihood
- Path of Thought
- Path of Action
- Path of Speech
- Path of Effort
- Path of Understanding
